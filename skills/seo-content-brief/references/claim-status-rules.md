# Claim Status Rules

| Allowed status | Permitted use |
| --- | --- |
| Verified for use | State the exact supported fact within its market, time, audience and scope. |
| Verified with qualification | Carry the qualification and any attribution or disclosure into each applicable section. |
| Supported inference | Label it as analysis, cite the premises, retain uncertainty and separate it from fact. |
| Approved attributable opinion or experience | Attribute it to the actual source or practitioner, preserve context and permission, and keep its status as opinion or experience explicit. |

Exclude Client confirmation required, Contradicted, Unsupported, Prohibited and Stale. Treat ambiguous, missing, expired or inaccessible support as unresolved. Equivalent labels are acceptable when the dossier explicitly establishes the same permission and evidence quality. Never promote a status from a plausible claim, competitor page, source summary, branding approval or user pressure.

For each material claim, retain the claim ID, source ID and exact source location, permitted meaning, use conditions, review trigger and approved attribution or disclosure. Reference the source register rather than reproducing it. Paraphrase source material when allowed. If required verbatim wording contains an em dash, refer to its exact source location and flag the wording requirement for review without altering the quotation. Keep unsupported items in a separate do-not-use list. If a necessary claim cannot be replaced by an approved, narrower statement without changing the page task, return to B2.

Check identity, version and geography on commercial facts. Confirm terms, eligibility, prices, availability, credentials, comparisons and results individually. Keep material conditions attached to numerical claims. A before-and-after observation does not establish cause or typical results. Any permitted quote needs its actual wording, context, speaker and permission. First-person use requires approved evidence of the attributed person's real contribution and permission for that voice. Source-led dossiers support source-led analysis.

For consequential safety, medical, financial, legal, reputational, comparative or commercial material, record the required named authorised or specialist reviewer, review scope and current status. If the role is known but the name is pending, state that explicitly. Block at B2 if the missing reviewer or clearance makes the evidence or planned contribution unusable. Keep required drafting clearance pending when an otherwise sound brief can be prepared for review.
