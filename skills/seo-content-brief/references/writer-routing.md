# Writer Routing

Choose the page's primary job. Supporting sections can serve secondary needs within the same route.

| Exact writer label | Primary reader job | Evidence to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| W1. Local Service Content Writer | Decide whether a real local service can help and what to do next. | Verify the service, operating coverage, any local proof and contact path. |
| W2. Affiliate Review and Comparison Writer | Evaluate independent affiliate reviews, comparisons, alternatives or a buying shortlist. | Require a fair comparison method, like-for-like scope, evidence of any actual testing, limitations and applicable disclosures. |
| W3. SaaS and B2B Content Writer | Evaluate a SaaS or B2B offer, feature, use case, integration, implementation or buyer decision. | Use approved capabilities, offer terms and applicability. Check results and security claims separately. |
| W4. Ecommerce Category and Collection Writer | Browse an available class of products and narrow the choice. | Verify assortment, selection criteria, useful attributes and legitimate collection boundaries. |
| W5. Ecommerce Product Detail Page Writer | Evaluate a specific product or meaningful variant group and its purchase fit. | Verify identity, specifications, variants, compatibility and relevant commercial terms. |
| W6. Expert-Led Article and Thought-Leadership Writer | Understand defensible research, an expert contribution, a case, or original source-led analysis. | Verify the method, provenance, actual contribution, permitted attribution and limits. |

A SaaS vendor's buyer page can be W3 even when it includes a comparison. An independent affiliate evaluation can be W2 even when the products are SaaS. A local firm's article can be W6 if credible analysis is the main job. A category buying guide can be W4 when it helps browse that collection. A specific product page remains W5 when education supports its purchase decision.

When the approved page job falls outside all six routes, return to B1. When a route fits but necessary supporting claims are unavailable, return to B2.
