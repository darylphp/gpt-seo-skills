# Input Requirements

Accept relevant extracts or equivalent approved records. Preserve their IDs or revisions where supplied.

| Upstream responsibility | Minimum usable input |
| --- | --- |
| A2. Brand Voice and Editorial Profile Builder | Applicable voice, point of view, market language, controlled terminology and claim or CTA restrictions. An explicitly approved minimal profile is enough. |
| A3. Keyword, Query, Intent and Entity Research | Approved primary query family, reader task, dominant intent and meaningful criteria or variants. Include compatible secondary intent when useful. |
| A4. Query-to-URL, Cluster and Roadmap Planning | Exact existing or proposed URL, page role, nearest competing or adjacent pages, owned and excluded topics, and approved internal-link routes where relevant. Record an explicit absence of a nearby page if established. |
| B1. Page Opportunity and Intent Validator | Page identifier, approved Create, Improve or Consolidate decision, audience, market, business objective, reader decision and outcome, and page-specific reason to proceed. Confirm any required consolidation approval, retained material and dependencies. |
| B2. Evidence and Claims Dossier Builder | Ready for B3, Ready for B3 as source-led, or equivalent approved page-specific dossier. Require permitted claims with IDs, traceable source IDs and locators, scope, evidence cut-off or dates, restrictions, limitations and asset permission where needed. |
| Production owner | Relevant format, exclusions, business constraints and review requirements. Capture approval role, specialist needs and any material unresolved decision. |

A bare keyword, competitor URL, raw source dump, an SEO score or a request to write a page is insufficient. Check that all records address the same URL, audience, market and revision. Preserve material disagreements and use the responsible owner to resolve them. A proposed future asset supplies no current proof.

## Return paths

- Return to B1 when the purpose, page action, scope, URL ownership or writer-route fit is missing or unresolved. Identify A2, A3 or A4 as the upstream owner where applicable. Route redirect-only, keep-only and out-of-scope requests back through the page decision without creating a brief.
- Return to B2 when page identity is settled but the dossier, core evidence, permission, differentiation proof or a required evidence-level specialist decision is missing, stale or conflicting.
- If both fail, return to B1 first and list the B2 dependency for the next pass.
- Optional evidence may be omitted when the remaining pack still serves the approved task and contribution. Record the omission and its effect.
- For a batch, ask the user to select one approved page if none is selected. Keep each brief and its approval record separate.
