# Brief Output Template

Use these 12 numbered headings in this order. Use one exact status from SKILL.md. For a blocked brief, show the failed gate and return action in section 1, retain available facts, and mark dependent sections Deferred. Section 12 states that there is no writer handoff.

## 1. Brief status and writer route

Record exact status, selected writer if ready, brief revision, page ID/action, dossier status and evidence cut-off, and the main limitation. Record Human approval: Awaiting approval unless the authorised approver, role, date and exact approved revision/scope are explicitly supplied. Include conditions and any required specialist role, named reviewer if known, and separate clearance state.

## 2. Page identity and boundary

Record the exact URL, role, page type, market/language, page job and approved action. List topics owned here, briefly answered and linked elsewhere, or excluded. Name the nearest URL and its distinct job. Include material to retain where applicable.

## 3. Reader, intent and success outcome

Record reader, situation, knowledge or concern, primary task and intent, decision, desired outcome and next step. Add compatible secondary intent where helpful. Define an observable sign the page meets its reader task.

## 4. Content promise and differentiation plan

State the baseline, observed common failure or its unknown status, one evidence-backed differentiator, optional supporting asset and practical consequence. Give a short planning-level promise covering audience, task, contribution and boundary.

## 5. Evidence and claims controls

List permitted claim and source IDs, approved meaning, status, section use, qualifications, attribution, disclosure and freshness or review conditions. List excluded and unresolved material separately as Do not use. Preserve source scope and accessible locators.

## 7. Original-asset and visual plan

Record asset ID, provenance, availability, purpose, section, permission, ownership, production dependency, accessible alternative and fallback when relevant. State none when the reader task needs no asset.

## 8. Query, entity and terminology rules

Record the primary family, compatible variants by meaning, decision-relevant entities and criteria, vocabulary choices and relevant exclusions.

## 9. Internal-link and CTA requirements

Record approved destinations or actions, reader purpose, placement, anchor or CTA concept and verified constraints. State when no suitable link or secondary action was approved.

## 10. Voice and natural-writing instructions

Record market spelling, point of view, authorised use of first person, tone, depth, formatting, controlled terms and relevant prohibitions.

## 11. Production requirements and acceptance tests

Record needed formats, evidence, review and accessibility requirements. Set observable tests for the future draft. Include the exact-revision approval gate and any specialist clearance. Include length guidance only for a justified production constraint.

## 12. Selected W-series writer handoff

Record one exact W-series label, brief and dossier references, page job and boundaries, differentiator, blueprint and evidence controls, approved assets, links, CTA and voice. Refer to sections above instead of duplicating them. Record conditions that require the writer to pause and return to the relevant owner. State whether drafting may begin under the recorded approvals.
