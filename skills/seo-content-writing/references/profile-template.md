# Profile Output Template

Use these 8 numbered headings in this order in profile mode, with one exact profile status from SKILL.md. For a blocked profile, name the missing sources and the smallest useful set in section 1, keep any confirmed facts in section 2, and mark sections 3 to 8 `Deferred`.

## 1. Status and handoff

The exact status on its own line. Then: profile ID and revision; brand; market and language variant; page types covered; and the main limitation in one sentence.

Then the approval line, the no-clearance line and the handoff line, each on its own line, as SKILL.md sets out.

Sources left out, and why.

## 3. Readers and their words

Who reads, what they already know, what worries them, the words they use, and where the brand's terms differ. Source IDs for each point.

## 5. Spelling, terms and formatting

Language variant, units, numbers, prices, dates and capitalisation. Controlled terms and terms to avoid. Heading, list and table conventions. CTA wording style.

## 6. Authorship and point of view

Who "we" is, when first person is allowed and for whom, bylines and reviewer lines, and what must never be implied without evidence.

## 7. Claims, phrases and habits to avoid

Prohibited claims as supplied, phrases removed in past corrections, habits to cut, and the library defaults.

## 8. Gaps and review triggers

Open questions, each with the smallest source that would settle it. When to review the profile, such as a new market, a rebrand, a change in the rules that apply to the brand's claims, or the same correction made more than once.
