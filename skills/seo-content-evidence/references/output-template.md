# Evidence Dossier Template

Use these 10 numbered headings in this order with one exact status from SKILL.md. For a blocked dossier, explain the gap in section 1, keep confirmed facts in section 2 and mark sections that depend on the missing input as `Deferred`. In targeted check mode, keep all 10 headings and mark sections outside the check as `Not applicable`. Keep long exports and raw source text in a referenced appendix, not in the narrative.

## 1. Status and summary

The exact status, then the direct conclusion in two or three sentences: what the page can claim, what it cannot, and what would change that. Name the page, the mode, the dossier ID and revision, the dossier date and the evidence cut-off. Give a count of claims by status. Then, each on its own line:

`Dossier approval: Awaiting approval`

`This dossier does not approve publication or any claim on the business's behalf.`

`Brief handoff: Ready for B3`

Replace the approval line only when an approval for this exact dossier revision was supplied, naming the approver, role, date and revision. Use the handoff line that matches the status, as listed in SKILL.md.

## 2. Page decision and scope

The URL or proposed URL, role, reader task, audience, market and language, the approved action with its approver and date, the upstream record ID, the boundary with the nearest page, the mode and its scope, and the owners for business facts, dossier approval and specialist review. In targeted check mode, the baseline wording and the change requested.

## 3. Sources

| ID | Source | Type | Version or date | Checked | Label | Locations used | Permitted use | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Then any source that was needed but is `Not verified`, and why.

## 4. Claim register

| ID | Proposed claim and intended meaning | Type | Where used | Source and location | Status | Conditions and qualifications | Confidence | Owner | Review needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

One row per claim or linked group. For an unusable claim, name the route (verify, narrow, qualify, replace, remove or defer) and the replacement claim ID if there is one.

## 5. Figures, quotes and comparisons

For each numerical claim: units, tax basis, denominator, sample, period, the recalculation and the conditions. For each quote: the original wording location, speaker, context and permission. For each comparison: the versions, conditions and criteria compared. Write `None in scope` where a group does not apply.

## 6. People, experience and review

Who did, tested, contributed to or reviewed what, for which revision, and what that supports. The first-person and experience claims the page may and may not make. Each regulated or high-consequence claim with its market, the qualified review it needs, the reviewer or role, and its status. AI assistance used in the supplied material, and what was checked.

## 7. Assets and permissions

| ID | Asset | Provenance | What it shows | Permission and scope | Privacy check | Use limits | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Write `No assets supplied` if there are none.

## 8. Conflicts, gaps and proof requests

Each conflict with both readings and how it was resolved or what would resolve it. Each inaccessible source. Then the proof requests:

| ID | Claim | Exact fact or asset needed | Why it matters | Acceptable evidence | Who can supply it | Can the page proceed without it? | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Then any research notes, kept apart from the claims.

## 9. Brief handoff

The basis: evidence dossier or source-led. The contribution the evidence can deliver, in one or two sentences. Then:

```text
Permitted claims: ID, permitted meaning, conditions, attribution or disclosure, source IDs
Do not use: ID, reason, replacement ID if any
Open proof requests: ID, effect on the page while open
Required reviews: claim ID, reviewer or role, status
Handoffs: page decision questions to site architecture; structured data facts to schema review; Business Profile facts to local SEO; release and live checks to publishing
```

For a targeted check, name the locations the editor must change and the claim IDs that support each change. For a blocked dossier, write `Deferred`.

## 10. Freshness and review

Each claim with a review trigger and its next check: price list revisions, effective dates, licence or certificate expiries, staff changes, policy or product updates and data periods. What evidence would change a status. Who owns the next review. No ranking, traffic, AI citation or timing promise.
